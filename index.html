Available names: home, heart, star, leaf and moon.

Make it your own

  1. Open the SVG Sprite Generator.
  2. Upload the five SVG files from this project, or use your own images.
  3. Export the sprite and use the generated HTML in your website.

These examples contain vector shapes, so they remain sharp at different display sizes. No separate 2× or 3× copies are needed for these icons.
